Game Memory

One of my favorite games as a kid was Bella Sara. It was a horse-themed online game where you could collect cards in real life that had art of magical horses on them. Each card had a unique code that you could enter online to unlock that horse in the game. I loved collecting the cards and playing the game to take care of my horses and explore the world. There was even a Nintendo DS version of the game that I also enjoyed playing. Even now as an adult, I still have some of my Bella Sara cards in a book that remind me of fond memories of playing the games.
