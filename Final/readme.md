Press space to pop the bubbles! I made an interactive art piece with Phaser. It was meant to be an aquarium where bubbles keep spawning and when you press space it pops them. I also drew the assets for the swimming fish and the seaweed! The bubbles, background, and platform are pulled from Phasers and the previous homework assignments assets. I used Google to figure out the audio for the bubbles. Music was sourced from Pixabay.

How I referenced the previous Phaser assignment: I used the player movement and sprite logic at first to get the fishes moving independently. The movement later changed so that I could have more control over the fishes' swim speed with user interaction. I used the original logic with collectibles to make the bubbles start out, then I built off of it to make more bubbles spawn. I used the same code that creates the platforms for the seaweed, just not the collision detection.

Where AI was used: helped to fix minor coding errors. Helped me to get the bubbles to float right. I realized that there was velocity for them to move on the y-axis afterward. I also used AI to resolve an issue with fish's swimming. I originally had the movement in the fishSwim function, but it was causing them to swim back too early. Using AI helped me understand the error and I moved their original velocity into the create function to resolve the issue. The fish movement after that then changed. Using AI helped me to build out how to make the fish swim faster when the bubbles are popped by using separate variables that gave me more control of the fish's speed.
